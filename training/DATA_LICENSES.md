## 1. Overview and Data Governance Rules

Per NeoGold's non-negotiable rules (`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/2_PRD.md` Section 11, `docs/4_System_Architecture.md` Section 11):
1. **Never commit raw data files:** Only manifests with cryptographic hashes (SHA-256) are committed to version control.
2. **Open redistribution license requirement:** No model derived from third-party data may be bundled into NeoGold or distributed publicly unless the underlying dataset's license explicitly grants training, derivative creation, and unrestricted open-source redistribution of the resulting model weights.
3. **Clinical safety and honesty:** If no openly licensed clinical dataset exists, the pipeline trains on bench phantom captures to prove bit-for-bit reproducibility and pipeline integrity. The resulting model card must state clearly that the model has **no clinical meaning**.

## 3. Decision for Canonical `model.json`

Because no public newborn clinical dataset with both:
1. Smartphone calibration card capture modality, and
2. Verified open redistribution license for derived clinical models

is available, NeoGold's canonical `model.json` is trained on **bench phantom captures** (`testdata/bench/`).

**Clinical Notice (PRD D11):**
> The model generated by `make reproduce` is trained on synthetic bench phantoms. It demonstrates mathematical and computational correctness of the feature extraction, ridge regression, and split-conformal calibration pipeline. It has **NO CLINICAL MEANING** and must not be used to direct patient care.
