# Task: Photo Keeps Failing

The photo keeps failing; get a result or find out why.

You are checking a 2-day-old full-term baby (mother agrees, no early jaundice, no danger signs, no prior phototherapy) right beside a bright ward window. Every time you try to take the photo, the app complains about the photo. Either get a final recommendation from the app or find out what action you are supposed to take next.
