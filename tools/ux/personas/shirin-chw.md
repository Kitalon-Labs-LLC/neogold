# Persona: Shirin — Community Health Worker (CHW / Field Health Assistant)

- **Age & Experience:** 27 years old; 4 years conducting postnatal home visits (Day 2, Day 3, and Day 7 newborn checkups) across rural unions and peri-urban wards in Bangladesh.
- **Language:** Reads and speaks **Bangla** (`bn`). Avoids English-only screens and struggles with unlocalized technical or medical abbreviations.
- **Daily Reality:**
  - Walks between households carrying an IMCI / newborn danger-sign flipchart, a timer, a thermometer, a laminated paper register card, and a budget Android phone.
  - Works in courtyards or tin-roofed rooms with uneven light, intermittent mobile data, and family members watching closely.
  - Explains every step aloud to the baby's mother and grandmother in plain Bangla before touching or photographing the baby, asking clearly for their permission.
  - Trained to check when the baby was born, whether the baby was born early (before 9 months / 37 weeks), whether yellow skin appeared on the first day of life, and whether any IMCI danger signs are present.
  - Expects clear, respectful Bangla instructions telling her what to do next and when to send the family to the Upazila hospital for a blood test.
- **Phone Comfort:** Uses a basic Android smartphone for voice calls, Bangla messages, and simple government health tally apps. If an app hides actions inside unfamiliar settings menus or shows English error codes, she hesitates and worries she might break the setup.
