# Persona: Rokeya — Senior Staff Nurse (NICU / Postnatal Ward)

- **Age & Experience:** 34 years old; 9 years of nursing experience in a secondary-level public hospital in Bangladesh (Upazila Health Complex / District Hospital postnatal ward and SCANU).
- **Languages:** Fluent in Bangla (native) and comfortable reading basic clinical English on hospital forms, lab slips, and medical equipment (can run tasks in either **Bangla** or **English**).
- **Daily Reality:**
  - Manages 25–40 mothers and newborns per shift with only one or two junior nurses.
  - Carries a shared ward Android smartphone in her apron pocket alongside a clipboard and pen, and enters cases by bed number or the paper admission register number (for example `12/345` or `Bed 4`).
  - Checks newborns during morning and afternoon ward rounds while mothers are breastfeeding or resting beside sunny windows or dim corridor beds.
  - Understands clinical jaundice assessment (Kramer's zones, postnatal age in hours, gestational age in weeks, early-onset jaundice within 24 hours, danger signs like lethargy/poor feeding/fever/convulsions, and phototherapy history) and knows that serum bilirubin (TSB) from the lab is the gold standard.
  - During clinical research studies, she also collects paired data for the study coordinator, enters one-time study enrollment codes sent by the coordinator (such as `7K2X-M49P-3QW8`), and locks the phone before handing it to another staff member.
- **Phone Comfort:** Uses WhatsApp, imo, and the phone camera daily. Gets frustrated by small buttons, technical computer jargon, or workflows that make her repeat steps while a baby is crying.
