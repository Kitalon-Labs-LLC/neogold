## 1. Objectives & Exit Criteria

1. **Unaided Screening (TC-USE-01):** $\ge 90\%$ of participants complete a full newborn screening journey (J2) unaided without verbal assistance.
2. **Teach-Back Interpretation (TC-USE-02):** $100\%$ of participants correctly articulate the clinical next step for all four possible triage outcomes.
3. **Critical Reassurance Hazard Prevention:** Zero participants mistake *"No further action from this screen"* for *"the baby is healthy / well"*.
4. **Bangla Wording Sign-Off (TC-USE-03):** 100% of Bangla UI strings, warnings, and mothers' counseling messages approved by the nurse panel and documented in `BANGLA_STRINGS_REVIEW.md`.

## 3. Standardized Task Protocols

### Task 1: Unaided Newborn Screening (Journey J2)
- **Scenario:** A mother brings her 3-day-old (72 hours), 39-week infant to the postnatal ward with mild jaundice. Guardian consent is given. No danger signs are present.
- **Goal:** Complete screening flow from Home screen (S09) through S10a, S10, S11-S13, S16 (card capture), and reach Result Screen (S20) within 60 seconds.
- **Pass Criteria:** Completed unaided, correct data entered, photo captured with card.

### Task 2: Handling Photo Retake Guidance
- **Scenario:** High window glare or tilted card position triggers quality gate rejection (S17).
- **Goal:** Nurse reads plain-language retake guidance ("Too much glare. Move away from window."), repositions the device, and recaptures successfully.
- **Pass Criteria:** Correct remedial action taken without facilitator hints.

### Task 3: Scheduled Recheck & Due List Navigation
- **Scenario:** The baby's result is *Recheck in 24 hours*.
- **Goal:** Nurse saves session to log (S21), navigates to Home screen (S09), locates the recheck due card, and explains when the infant must be re-evaluated.
- **Pass Criteria:** Correctly identifies due time and explains that Home screen works offline without notifications.

### Task 4: Research Mode Blinded Capture Protocol
- **Scenario:** Enrolled study session for partner clinical validation.
- **Goal:** Nurse unlocks research mode with PIN, enters Participant Code `SKP-0142` and Consent ID `SKP-C-0142`, records 4-site protocol photos (Sternum, Forehead, Sclera), and inputs paired TSB laboratory value.
- **Pass Criteria:** Acknowledges that no estimate is shown (blinded capture), session saved to encrypted storage.

## 5. "What to Tell the Mother" Script (TC-UI-09, NG-047)

Nurses test reading the counseling card directly to the mother:

> **Bangla (বাংলা):**  
> "আপনার শিশুর গায়ের রঙ ও চোখের হলুদ ভাব আমরা ফোনের ক্যামেরায় একটি বিশেষ কার্ড ব্যবহার করে পরীক্ষা করেছি। এটি রক্ত পরীক্ষা নয়, তবে রক্ত পরীক্ষা লাগবে কি না তা বুঝতে সাহায্য করে।  
> আজকের পরীক্ষার ফলাফল অনুযায়ী: [স্ক্রিনের ফলাফল পড়ুন]।  
> শিশু যদি ঠিকমতো দুধ না খায়, অতিরিক্ত ঘুমায়, শরীর ধনুকের মতো বেঁকে যায়, অথবা জন্ডিস আরও বাড়ে—তবে দেরি না করে এখনই হাসপাতালে নিয়ে আসবেন।"

> **English:**  
> "We screened your baby's skin color using a phone camera and a special medical calibration card. This is not a blood test, but it helps us decide if a blood test is needed.  
> Based on today's screening: [read screen action].  
> If your baby stops feeding well, becomes excessively sleepy, arches backwards, or appears more yellow, bring the baby to the hospital immediately."
