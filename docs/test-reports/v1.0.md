## 0. Mandatory Clinical Disclaimer

> [!CAUTION]
> **Software testing cannot show that NeoGold is clinically safe.**
>
> Software verification and validation confirm that the algorithmic pipeline, device qualification, data structures, cryptographic engines, and security controls perform deterministically and adhere to specifications.
>
> Software testing **does not** prove clinical efficacy, diagnostic sensitivity/specificity on human newborns, or absence of clinical risk. Clinical validity requires partner validation under Institutional Ethics Committee approval in Bangladesh with paired total serum bilirubin (TSB) laboratory reference standards.
>
> Because `triageEnabled` is `false` in this v1.0 Closed Research Release (Path B, PRD D10), the app operates in blinded data collection mode and displays **no triage actions or diagnostic recommendations**.

## 7. What Testing Does NOT Show

1. **Software testing cannot show that NeoGold is clinically safe.** Testing demonstrates algorithmic correctness against synthetic and bench data. Clinical accuracy on Bangladeshi newborns is evaluated exclusively by the partner validation study.
2. **Testing on matrix phones does not guarantee optical consistency on unlisted third-party devices.** The in-app Camera Qualification engine (FR-04) verifies each phone individually before enabling screening.
3. **Offline laboratory tests do not prove hospital Wi-Fi stability.** NeoGold's offline-first architecture, local encrypted Room database, and store-and-forward sync ensure no research data is lost when hospital networks are down.
