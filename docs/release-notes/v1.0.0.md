| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| App version | `1.0.0` (`versionCode` 1000001) |
| Target track | Google Play closed testing only (PRD D10, doc 12 Section 4.3) |
| Triage status | Off. The shipped config has `triageEnabled: false`; no screening result is shown to a worker |
| Model | `1.0.0-synthetic` (trained on bench phantoms; no clinical meaning, `MODEL_CARD.md`) |

Install only at study sites with the partner's ethics approval in place. NeoGold is decision support for trained health workers; it suggests a next step and the health worker decides. It does not diagnose.

---

## 1. What this build does

- **Research mode.** Unlock with a PIN (Argon2id, bound to the phone's hardware key). Enter a participant code and consent ID, take the blinded captures with the NeoGold card (the estimate is never shown, in the accessibility tree or in a log), enter the paired blood test value now or later, and complete the session.
- **Study server.** Join a study server by scanning the signed study QR and entering the one-time code. Items upload by themselves over Wi-Fi after a save, after enrolling and at app start, or on demand with "Upload now". Leaving the server clears this phone's device key and ID; the study and its rows stay, so export and retention keep working.
- **Encrypted export.** Export the study to a USB or storage location as an encrypted file; it opens only with the study's offline decrypt tool.
- **Local protection.** SQLCipher database, encrypted research photos, no analytics or crash SDKs, no names, birth dates, phone numbers or locations stored, delete-all-data with restart, retention that asks the server first.
- **Screening flow (built, hidden).** Inputs, the engine's hard rules, card check, camera, quality gate, estimate, the optional forehead photo and the suggested next step are built and tested on a computer. They stay off in this build.

## 2. What is verified, and how

Verified by automated tests and gates on a computer and in CI: the engines (triage, estimation, colour science, card trust), the screening flow, research session, export round trip, sync against a mock server, the release gates (signed config, manifest, native imports, 16 KB alignment, licences, dependency and secret scans).

## 3. Known limits

- Bangla wording is a draft awaiting the nurse panel.
- The shipped fonts are the phone's own; Noto fonts are not bundled yet.
- Gestational age first tap starts at 38 weeks (BUG-2026-083 item 5).
- The clock-offset checks of research mode (H2) are not in the research capture screens yet (BUG-2026-083 item 6).
- After leaving the study server, local rows are kept until the retention period; no earlier removal (BUG-2026-083 item 34).

## 4. Not verified on a phone (owner)

Camera capture and live detection; the whole research session; real enrollment and upload to a staging server over HTTPS; WorkManager conditions (Wi-Fi, battery); the minified release build; the device matrix D1 to D7 (kill and power-pull runs); the lock-screen behaviour of the export; the nurse panel and usability runs (TC-USE-01 to 05).
