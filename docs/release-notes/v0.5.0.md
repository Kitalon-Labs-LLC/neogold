## 1. What's New in v0.5.0

NeoGold v0.5.0 is the first integrated release candidate for partner study sites under Institutional Ethics Committee approval. This build operates exclusively in **Research Mode** with diagnostic triage results blinded, supporting the partner clinical validation protocol in Bangladesh.

- **Blinded Capture Workflow:** Guided camera capture using the physical NeoGold card, automatic quality gating (blur, glare, tilt, non-uniformity rejection), and zero clinical reassurance or triage displays.
- **Paired TSB Entry:** Health workers record paired total serum bilirubin (TSB) laboratory values with unlinked session tracking.
- **Local Data Protection:** SQLCipher 4 page encryption for structured records, Tink streaming AEAD (`AES256_GCM_HKDF_4KB`) for research photos, and argon2id PIN verifier bound to hardware Keystore.
- **Secure Sync & Export:** Background store-and-forward outbox with TLS 1.3, request signing via hardware ECDSA P-256 keys, and X25519 HPKE encrypted USB export.
- **Platform Hardening:** 16 KB page-aligned native binaries, Android 16 (API 36) targetSdk, no cleartext traffic permitted, and cloud backup disabled.
